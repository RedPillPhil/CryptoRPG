Bagholderz Odyssey - Web3 RPG

What is Bagholderz Odyssey?

Bagholderz Odyssey is a Web3 play-to-earn RPG built on the Base blockchain, centered around the hilariously painful journey of a lifelong bagholder. Explore a rich pixel-art world where players can earn $BAGZ and many other partner tokens from the Base chain, all while navigating the chaotic highs and lows of DeFi life.

What is the Bagz Token?

$BAGZ launched fairly on Mint.club with a humble $30 market cap — no presale, no insiders, no VC wallets. Just a clean, fair launch so everyone could ape in equally. It's the lifeblood of the in-game economy, used for everything from minting characters to buying lands, to tossing it all at the roulette wheel.

How do I earn $BAGZ?

Simple: Play. Earn. Repeat. Mint your unique character NFT with $BAGZ, then head into the world to farm, stake at the in-game bank, rent land, trade goods, or hit the casino. The more you play, the more you can earn — all while living your best (or worst) crypto life.

What is BagzSwap?

BagzSwap is the decentralized exchange of the Bagholderz Odyssey ecosystem. It lets you trade $BAGZ and other ecosystem tokens — and will soon serve as the backbone for NFT item sales and future partner integrations. If you're swapping, you're swapping on BagzSwap.

What is Ditto?

Ditto is an experimental rebase token that serves as the "gold standard" of the Bagholderz ecosystem. It launches with just 100 tokens and a $1,000 market cap. Every day, the supply rebases upwards — no negative rebases, no punishment for holding. Instead, the transaction burn rate algorithmically increases over time, preserving value while discouraging swing trading. Holders of $BAGZ will receive Ditto airdrops proportional to their holdings. While $BAGZ powers the game economy, Ditto is the store of value — a token designed to grow with the ecosystem.

Think of it like this: $BAGZ is the in-game cash — fluid, fast, fun. Ditto is the gold — rare, valuable, and slow to move. In-game fees may be auto-swapped into Ditto via smart contracts, creating natural demand. It’s not for spending — it’s for holding. Strong hands will be rewarded.

Who is Bagzzie?

Bagzzie is your in-game AI sidekick and resident therapist. Outside the game, he lives on the website as a clickable AI chat assistant. Inside the game, he powers our dynamic NPC conversations — offering tips, stories, or just vibing. If the NPCs seem too smart to be real… that’s because they are.
